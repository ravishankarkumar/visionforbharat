## Migration note

Imported as an unpublished research seed from the earlier `nation-first` repository. It requires technical, privacy, competition, security, and international-regulatory research before publication.

## In brief

Email allows a person using one provider to reach a person using another. Most instant-messaging networks instead require both people to use the same service. This seed asks whether open interoperability standards could give users a meaningful choice of provider while preserving secure communication.

The mechanism cannot simply demand universal data sharing. Any credible design must address end-to-end encryption, consent, spam, abuse, metadata, identity, smaller providers, and the danger of freezing innovation into a mandated standard.
