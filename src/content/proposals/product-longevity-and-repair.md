## Migration note

Imported as an unpublished research seed from the earlier `nation-first` repository. Research is needed on present consumer law, repair markets, safety, intellectual property, e-waste, software support, and effects on price and innovation.

## In brief

The seed concerns products that become unusable because parts, repair information, compatible consumables, batteries, or software support are unavailable. Rather than assuming every short product life is deliberate, the proposal should examine measurable obligations: support-period disclosure, access to parts and manuals, repairability information, and proportionate durability standards.
