## In brief

This seed asks whether a corridor around Hajipur and Bhagwanpur could support technology services, education, health, and associated enterprises. The work must begin with evidence about land, flooding and drainage, transport, power, digital connectivity, skills, existing industry, environmental constraints, and the risk of speculative development displacing local communities.
