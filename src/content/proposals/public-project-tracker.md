## Migration note

Imported as an unpublished research seed from the earlier `nation-first` repository. It needs a careful definition of covered projects, reporting responsibility, data quality, commercial confidentiality, and consequences for non-compliance.

## In brief

A stable public project record could connect the sanctioned purpose, responsible authority, planned cost, contractor, delivery milestones, payments, changes, delays, and completion evidence. The design should make ordinary scrutiny easier without turning reporting into an administrative burden that overwhelms smaller local works.
