## Migration note

Imported as an unpublished Bihar research seed from the earlier `nation-first` repository. It needs a verified inventory, conservation constraints, transport analysis, local consultation, and a clear relationship with existing tourism programmes.

## In brief

Bihar’s religious sites, forts, archaeological remains, hills, landscapes, and industrial heritage can be understood as connected journeys. A credible proposal would focus not only on promotion but also on preservation, interpretation, local guides and enterprises, sanitation, accessibility, safety, and dependable movement between sites.
