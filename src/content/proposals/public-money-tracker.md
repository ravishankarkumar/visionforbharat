Imported as an unpublished research seed from the earlier `nation-first` repository. It must be reconciled with existing Union, state, audit, procurement, and open-data systems before publication.

## In brief

Budget announcements, departmental releases, procurement records, transfers to implementing bodies, and final expenditure often appear in different systems and formats. This idea asks what it would take to give every public rupee a traceable, comprehensible path without pretending that one database can replace constitutional audit or financial control.

Related ideas could explore parliamentary and legislative constituency funds, contractor disclosure, and project-level delivery tracking.
