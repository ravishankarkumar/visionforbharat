## In brief

Street waste, unsafe transport of garbage, polluted water bodies, spitting, blocked drainage, and waterlogging are connected by weak systems but are not one policy problem. They could be explored separately through collection, transport, disposal, drainage, enforcement, worker safety, and visible service standards.
