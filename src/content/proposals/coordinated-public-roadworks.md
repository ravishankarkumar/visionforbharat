## In brief

New and resurfaced roads are sometimes cut again when another agency needs to install or repair water, power, telecommunications, drainage, or other infrastructure. The public experiences the agencies as one state even when their plans and budgets are separate.

The proposal is to require a shared planning window before major road construction, publish known utility requirements, and design accessible service corridors where feasible. Emergency work must remain possible, but predictable work should be coordinated before the surface is completed.
